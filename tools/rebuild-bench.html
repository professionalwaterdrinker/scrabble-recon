Game rebuild bench

Quackle plays games against itself, picking one of its top plays the way a decent club player might. Each finished board is then rebuilt and compared with the real order of play. Add ?games=6 to change how many games run, ?old to also score a previous solver saved as tools/old-solver.tmp.js (for example with git show HEAD~1:js/solver.js). Not part of the app.
