Position

One instruction per line. move <player> <place> <word> replays a play (players count from 0, 8H is across from H8, H8 is down, . is a letter already on the board, lowercase is a blank). rack is the rack to analyse, actual is the play that was really made.

Quackle's view

#WhereWordPtsEquityKeeps

Equity is Quackle's estimate of a play's worth: its score plus the value of the tiles kept for the next turn, using Quackle's English leave values. This is Quackle's quick static evaluation, not its slower simulation.
